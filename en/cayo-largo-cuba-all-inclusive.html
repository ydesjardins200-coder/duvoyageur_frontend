Practical guide

Planning your Cayo Largo trip

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Hurricanes mainly from August to October. Cayo Largo is tiny and unspoiled: people come here for the beach and the tranquillity.

What to do there?

  • Playa Sirena & Playa Paraíso — two of Cuba's most beautiful beaches, with powdery white sand.
  • Turtle farm — the island's sea turtle protection centre.
  • Iguanas & birds — the island is home to protected wildlife, including iguanas and pelicans.
  • Snorkelling & diving — unspoiled reefs all around the island.
  • Cayo Iguana — boat trip to an islet inhabited by iguanas.

Real questions

Cayo Largo: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Cayo Largo?

About 3 h 45 on a direct flight (mostly in winter). The airport is on the island, so the transfer is minimal (10 min).

What documents do I need for Cuba?

A passport (6 months recommended), an e-visa from the official site evisacuba.cu (often included) and the D'Viajeros form within 72 hours before departure. Medical insurance is mandatory. See our Passport and travel documents guide.

Who is Cayo Largo for?

For lovers of deserted beaches and tranquillity. The island is small and lightly developed, and nature rules here.

Is it safe right now?

Canada has advised "a high degree of caution" in Cuba since January 2026 (shortages). We help you choose a reliable resort and stay transparent.
