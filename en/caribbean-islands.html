Real questions

Travelling the French Caribbean: what people ask us

Before you choose your island, here are answers to the questions we hear most often.

Which French-speaking Caribbean islands should I visit?

Our three favourites: Saint Martin, Guadeloupe and Martinique. These are French-speaking islands known for being safe and reasonably priced, with superb beaches and a real change of scenery.

Is French spoken on these islands?

Yes. Guadeloupe and Martinique are French territories where French is the official language, and the French side of Saint Martin is French-speaking too. Handy for travelling with peace of mind.

What documents do I need to go there from Québec?

A valid Canadian passport is required. Since Guadeloupe and Martinique are French territories, no visa is needed for a short tourist stay. Always check the current entry requirements before you leave.

When is the best time to visit the Caribbean?

December to April is the dry season: the best weather. The rainy and hurricane season runs mostly from August to October — keep that in mind if you book for that period.
