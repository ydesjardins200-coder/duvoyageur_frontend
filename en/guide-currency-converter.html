Quick reference

💵 The US dollar is king. At all-inclusives, tips, excursions and taxis are almost always paid in US dollars. Bring small bills (US$1 and US$5) — they're handier than pesos for tipping, and you'll avoid bad exchange rates on site.

Rates are indicative, for estimating tips and small purchases. Actual rates (ATM, card, exchange office) vary and often include fees. For large amounts, check with your bank.
