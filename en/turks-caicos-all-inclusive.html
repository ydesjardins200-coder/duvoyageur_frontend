Practical guide

Planning your trip to Turks & Caicos

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. The climate is sunny and dry for much of the year. Hurricanes mostly from August to October.

What to do there?

  • Grace Bay Beach — 18 km of white sand and turquoise water, often voted the world's most beautiful beach.
  • Snorkelling & diving — the third-largest coral reef system in the world, with spectacular reefs.
  • Cay sandbar & Iguana Island — boat excursions to deserted islets.
  • Chalk Sound — a surreal turquoise lagoon, by kayak or paddleboard.
  • Conch — the local specialty, to try at the Conch Farm or by the sea.

The real questions

Turks & Caicos: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Turks & Caicos?

About 4 h 30 on a direct flight (mainly in winter) to Providenciales (PLS), then 15–30 min to Grace Bay.

What documents do I need for Turks & Caicos?

A passport valid for the length of your stay (for Canadians) and a return ticket. No visa (up to 90 days). See our Documents and passport guide.

Why is it more expensive?

Turks & Caicos is a premium, exclusive destination. The beauty of Grace Bay makes it one of the most sought-after spots in the Caribbean — and your discount helps lighten the bill.

What's the best time to go?

December to April, for the dry season. See our Best time to go guide.
