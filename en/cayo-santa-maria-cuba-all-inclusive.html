Practical guide

Planning your Cayo Santa María trip

When to go?

December to April for the best weather (dry season). Hurricanes mostly from August to October. The 48 km sea causeway to the cayo is an attraction in itself.

What to do there?

  • The cayo's beach — white sand and turquoise water, often nearly deserted.
  • Pueblo La Estrella — a small tourist village with bars, shops and shows.
  • Day trip to Santa Clara — Che Guevara's historic city, on the mainland.
  • Snorkelling — colourful reefs to explore along the coast.
  • Catamaran and sandbanks — a day at sea to the nearby islets.

Real questions

Cayo Santa María: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most frequently asked questions.

How long are the flight and transfer?

About 3 h 45 to Santa Clara, then a 1 h to 1 h 30 drive along the long sea causeway to the cayo.

What documents do I need for Cuba?

Passport (6 months' validity recommended), e-visa via evisacuba.cu (often included) and D'Viajeros within 72 h before departure. Medical insurance is mandatory. See our Documents and passport guide.

Do I need cash?

Yes — bring small bills in CAD or USD, since cards often don't work well in Cuba.

Is it safe right now?

Canada has advised a "high degree of caution" since January 2026 (shortages that can affect resorts). We'll help you choose a reliable hotel.
