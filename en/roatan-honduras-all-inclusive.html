Practical guide

Planning your trip to Roatán

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Diving and snorkelling on the Mesoamerican Reef are superb almost year-round. Hurricanes mainly from August to October.

What to do there?

  • West Bay Beach — one of the most beautiful beaches in the Caribbean, with the reef just a few strokes from shore.
  • Diving & snorkelling — the Mesoamerican Reef, the 2nd largest in the world, right there.
  • West End — the laid-back village, with its restaurants and easygoing nightlife.
  • Sloth & dolphin sanctuaries — close encounters with local wildlife.
  • Jungle zipline — fly over the island's canopy.

The real questions

Roatán: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Roatán?

About 5 h 30, on a seasonal or connecting flight to Roatán's airport (RTB), then 15–30 min to West Bay.

What documents do I need for Honduras / Roatán?

A passport valid for 6 months beyond departure, and no visa for Canadians (up to 90 days). See our Documents and passport guide.

Is Roatán safe?

Roatán and the Bay Islands are the tourist and diving area, noticeably safer than the mainland. We recommend staying in the resort area and booking excursions with reputable operators.

Who is Roatán for?

Divers and snorkellers, and anyone looking for a dream beach (West Bay) in a laid-back setting.
