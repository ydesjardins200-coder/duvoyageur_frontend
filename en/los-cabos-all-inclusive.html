Practical guide

Planning your trip to Los Cabos

When to go?

Los Cabos is very sunny and dry almost all year. From November to April the weather is ideal (and it's whale season, from December to April). Summer is very hot, with some rain from August to October.

What to do there?

  • El Arco & Land's End — the iconic rock arch, best seen by boat or kayak.
  • Whale watching — from December to April, one of Mexico's best whale-watching destinations.
  • Playa del Amor — "Lover's Beach," reachable by water taxi.
  • Cabo San Lucas Marina — bars, restaurants and the starting point for boat excursions.
  • San José del Cabo — its art gallery district and quiet colonial charm.

The real questions

Los Cabos: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Los Cabos?

About 6 hours on a direct flight (mostly in winter), then a 30–45 min drive depending on whether you're staying in Cabo San Lucas or San José del Cabo.

Can you swim in Los Cabos?

Heads up: many beaches have strong currents and aren't safe for swimming. We'll steer you toward resorts with a swimmable beach (e.g. Hyatt Ziva, Médano) if that matters to you.

When can you see the whales?

From December to April — Los Cabos is one of the best places in the world for it.

Is there sargassum?

No, it's the Pacific. And the climate is desert-like: very dry and sunny.
