The real questions

Adults-only all-inclusives: what people ask us

What is an "adults-only" all-inclusive?

A resort (or a section of a resort) reserved for adults — usually 18 and over. No kids, so the atmosphere is calmer and often more romantic: quiet pools, refined restaurants and evening shows.

What's the minimum age?

Most often 18, but some brands set the limit at 16 or 21 depending on the hotel. We'll confirm the exact age for the resort you're interested in before you book.

Which ones for couples, which ones for friends?

For a honeymoon or a romantic stay: Secrets, Excellence, Sandals or Couples. For a more festive vibe with friends: TRS, Royalton CHIC or Breathless. Tell us your vibe and we'll point you in the right direction.

How do I get the best price?

Search for the hotel and dates in our booking engine: your discount is already applied to the tour operators' price. No obligation — book whenever you like.
