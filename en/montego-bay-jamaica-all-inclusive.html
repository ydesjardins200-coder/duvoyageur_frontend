Practical guide

Planning your trip to Montego Bay

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Hurricanes are mainly a risk from August to October. Montego Bay is lively all year round.

What to do there?

  • Doctor's Cave Beach — the city's iconic beach, perfect for swimming.
  • Rose Hall Great House — a haunted colonial mansion, best visited in the evening.
  • Martha Brae rafting — a peaceful bamboo-raft ride down the river.
  • Sunset cruise — catamaran, music and snorkelling off MoBay.
  • Margaritaville — the festive waterfront hot spot, with waterslides and a great vibe.

The real questions

Montego Bay: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Montego Bay?

About 4 h 45 non-stop (offered mainly in winter). The airport is right in town, so the transfer is very short.

What documents do I need for Jamaica?

A valid passport and the online "Enter Jamaica" form (C5), to be filled out before departure on the official site enterjamaica.gov.jm. See our Documents and passport guide.

Montego Bay or Negril?

MoBay is more convenient (airport in town, golf, nightlife); Negril has Seven Mile Beach. Both are superb — we'll help you choose based on your style.

What's the best time to go?

December to April, for the dry season. See our Best time to go guide.
