The real questions

Luxury resorts down south: what people ask us

Before you pick your luxury all-inclusive, here are the answers to the questions we hear most often.

What are the best luxury all-inclusive resorts down south?

Our current favourites: Hotel Xcaret and the Secrets collection (Maroma, Akumal, Moxché) on the Riviera Maya, Atelier Playa Mujeres and the Iberostar's Coral Level in Cancún, TRS Coral in Costa Mujeres, and Secrets Cap Cana in the Dominican Republic. They combine upscale service, stunning beaches and a premium all-inclusive concept.

Do I pay more by going through a discount agency?

No, it's the opposite. You pay less than the price listed by the tour operator, because we give you back part of our commission as a discount, applied right on your price.

When is the best time to head down south?

December to April is the dry season: the best weather, but also the highest prices. From May to early June, you'll often find better rates and fewer crowds — perfect for a laid-back getaway.

Are luxury all-inclusives adults-only or open to families?

Both. Some are adults-only (like Secrets and TRS Coral), perfect for couples. Others welcome families (like Hotel Xcaret with its parks included). Tell us who's travelling and we'll point you in the right direction.

How do I get my discounted price?

Create your free account, search for your package in our booking engine and you'll see your price right away, discount already applied. You book it yourself, whenever you want.

Is it safe to book with Du Voyageur?

Yes. We hold Québec travel agency licence no. 700495 and we book through the official Softvoyage network. Your booking is a real booking with the tour operator (Transat, Air Canada Vacations, Sunwing, WestJet).
