Practical guide

Planning your trip to Bayahibe

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Hurricanes mainly from August to October. Bayahibe is an excellent starting point for boat excursions.

What to do there?

  • Saona Island — the star excursion: white sand, turquoise water and a natural pool.
  • Catalina Island — a snorkelling and diving paradise.
  • Cotubanamá National Park — trails, caves and cenotes near Bayahibe.
  • Bayahibe village — colourful lanes, small seafood spots and fishing boats.
  • Scuba diving — renowned sites, including wrecks that beginners can reach.

The real questions

Bayahibe: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How do you get to Bayahibe?

Fly into La Romana (≈ 4 h 15 from Montréal, mainly in winter), then a 20–30 min drive. Some packages also go through Punta Cana (≈ 1 h drive).

What documents do I need for the Dominican Republic?

A valid passport and the online "E-Ticket" form before arrival. See our Documents and passport guide.

Who is Bayahibe for?

For travellers who want peace and quiet, beautiful beaches and easy access to Saona and Catalina — far from the bustle of Punta Cana.

What's the best time to go?

December to April, for the dry season. See our Best time to go guide.
