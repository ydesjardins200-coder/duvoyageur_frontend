Practical guide

Planning your Curaçao trip

When to go?

Curaçao is a year-round destination: little rain, lots of sun and, above all, OUTSIDE the hurricane belt. Like Aruba and Bonaire, the island enjoys a very pleasant dry, breezy climate.

What to do there?

  • Willemstad — the UNESCO-listed capital with its colourful façades and floating bridge.
  • Playa Kenepa & Cas Abao — white-sand, turquoise-water coves among the most beautiful anywhere.
  • Snorkelling & diving — reefs you can reach right from shore, all over the island.
  • Hato Caves — stalactites and bats, a cool place to visit.
  • Christoffel Park — hiking, wildlife and the island's highest peak.

Real questions

Curaçao: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most frequently asked questions.

Why choose Curaçao?

For its turquoise coves, its colourful UNESCO-listed capital and its location OUTSIDE the hurricane belt — a dry, sunny climate all year long.

What documents do I need for Curaçao?

A valid passport and the Digital Immigration Card, to be filled out online within 7 days before departure on the official website dicardcuracao.com. See our Documents and passport guide.

Is Curaçao all-inclusive?

Many hotels aren't all-inclusive. We find you the real all-inclusive options (Dreams, Sunscape, Zoëtry) and compare prices.

Aruba or Curaçao?

Aruba has the longest sandy beaches; Curaçao has more character (Willemstad) and wild coves. We'll help you choose.
