Practical guide

Getting ready for your Costa Mujeres trip

When to go?

December to April for the dry season, the best weather and less sargassum. Seaweed is more common from May to August. Hurricanes mainly from August to October.

What to do there?

  • Costa Mujeres beach — long, calm, lightly developed beaches, perfect for walking.
  • Isla Mujeres — the neighbouring island, with its beaches and golf carts, by ferry.
  • Snorkelling at MUSA — the underwater sculpture museum off Isla Mujeres.
  • Cancún — the hotel zone and its nightlife, 40 min to the south.
  • Cenotes & Chichén Itzá — nature and culture excursions in the Yucatán.

The real questions

Costa Mujeres: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

Where is Costa Mujeres?

Just north of Cancún: ≈ 4 h 30 flight to Cancún, then ≈ 40 min by road. It's a newer, quiet area.

What documents do I need for Mexico?

A valid passport (no visa for Canadians), the FMM form on arrival and the Quintana Roo Visitax online. See our Documents and passport guide.

Is it good for families?

Yes — the resorts are recent, with big water parks and kids' clubs, on long, calm beaches.

Is there sargassum?

Possibly from May to August. From December to April, the beaches are generally cleaner.
