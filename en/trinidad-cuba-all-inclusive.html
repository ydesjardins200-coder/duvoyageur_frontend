Practical guide

Getting ready for your trip to Trinidad

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Trinidad is a year-round destination; the town is mainly a cultural discovery trip, usually combined with Playa Ancón beach.

What to do there?

  • Trinidad's historic centre — cobblestone streets, Plaza Mayor and colourful colonial houses (UNESCO).
  • Casa de la Música — open-air live music on the steps in the evening.
  • Valle de los Ingenios — old sugar plantations, by train or taxi.
  • Playa Ancón — the most beautiful beach on the south coast, 15 min away.
  • Topes de Collantes park — waterfalls and hikes in the Escambray mountains.

The real questions

Trinidad: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

Is Trinidad a beach destination?

It's first and foremost a cultural discovery destination (UNESCO colonial town). Combine it with Playa Ancón beach, 15 min away, to get the best of both.

How do I get there?

Fly to Cienfuegos or Santa Clara (≈ 3 hrs 45 from Montréal, mostly in winter), then a 1 hr to 1 hr 20 drive.

What documents do I need for Cuba?

A passport (6 months' validity recommended), an e-visa via evisacuba.cu (often included) and D'Viajeros within 72 hours before departure. Medical insurance is mandatory. See our Documents and passport guide.

Is it safe right now?

Since January 2026, Canada has advised a high degree of caution in Cuba (shortages). We stay transparent and help you plan carefully.
