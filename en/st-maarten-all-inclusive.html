Practical guide

Planning your St. Martin / Sint Maarten trip

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Hurricanes mostly from August to October. The island is as much about the table as the beach: Grand-Case is the culinary capital of the Caribbean.

What to do there?

  • Maho Beach — the beach where planes land right over your head: one of a kind.
  • Grand-Case (French side) — the “culinary capital of the Caribbean” and its lolos (local grills).
  • Orient Bay — the island's most beautiful beach, lively and chic.
  • Marigot & Philipsburg — the two capitals, with markets, duty-free shops and waterfronts.
  • Pinel & Tintamarre — paradise islets just a hop away by shuttle or boat.

The real questions

St. Martin / Sint Maarten: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to St. Maarten?

About 4 h 45 non-stop (mostly in winter) to SXM airport (Dutch side), followed by a short transfer.

What documents do I need for St. Martin / Sint Maarten?

A valid passport (no visa for Canadians) and the Sint Maarten online entry form via entry.sx, to fill out before departure. See our Documents and passport guide.

French side or Dutch side?

The French side (Saint-Martin) is quieter and more of a foodie spot; the Dutch side (Sint Maarten) is livelier (casino, Maho Beach). You can move freely between the two.

When is the best time to go?

December to April for the dry season. See our Best time to go guide.
