Practical guide

Getting ready for your trip to Mexico

When to go?

The dry season, from December to April, has the best weather — and it's also the most popular. Hurricane season runs from June to November. Good to know: sargassum seaweed can wash up on Caribbean coast beaches, mostly from spring to fall; how much varies from one week and one beach to the next.

What to do there?

  • Chichén Itzá — one of the wonders of the Mayan world, best seen on an excursion.
  • Cenotes — a magical freshwater swim in turquoise caves.
  • Tulum — Mayan ruins perched above a Caribbean beach.
  • Isla Mujeres — catamaran, snorkelling and dreamy beaches.
  • Xcaret & Xel-Há parks — nature, culture and activities for the whole family.

The real questions

Cancún vacations: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive in Cancún or on the Riviera Maya, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Cancún?

About 4.5 to 5 hours on a direct flight, from Montréal's airport (YUL) to Cancún's (CUN). It's one of the most popular sun destinations from Québec.

What documents do I need to go to Cancún?

A valid Canadian passport (ideally for the length of your stay). No visa is required for tourism (stays up to 180 days), and a tourist card (FMM) is usually issued on arrival or included in your package. Always check the latest requirements before you go.

What's the best time to go, and what about sargassum?

December to April is the dry season with the best weather. Hurricane season runs from June to November. Sargassum seaweed can wash up on Caribbean coast beaches, mostly from spring to fall — it's unpredictable and varies by week and by beach. We can point you to areas that are often less affected.

What currency should I use in Cancún?

The official currency is the Mexican peso (MXN), but US dollars and credit cards are widely accepted at resorts. Let your bank know before you leave if you plan to use your cards.

What are the best all-inclusives in Cancún and Riviera Maya?

Among our favourites: Atelier Playa Mujeres (high-end), plus Iberostar Selection Paraíso and Barceló Maya for families. We also have Riu Palace Mexico, Grand Riviera Princess and many more in our top picks.
