Practical guide · Updated 2026

Passport and travel documents for down south

Everything you need to clear customs with no surprises — by destination. Passport, E-Ticket, immigration forms, insurance and taxes.

⚠️ Rules can change. This page covers the up-to-date essentials (2026), but always check the official source — travel.gc.ca (official Government of Canada advisories) — before you leave.

🧳 For every destination

  • Valid passportfor your entire stay — we recommend 6 months of validity after your return to avoid being turned away.
  • Travel insurance with medical coveragemandatory in the Dominican Republic, strongly recommended everywhere. It is not included in packages: buy it separately or check your credit card's coverage, and keep the emergency number handy.
  • Return ticket / proof of onward travel
  • Copy of your passporta photo in the cloud + a paper copy, just in case.
  • Kidsneed their own passport. If only one parent is travelling, a consent letter from the other parent is recommended.

📍 By destination

Dominican Republic

Punta Cana · La Romana · Bayahibe · Puerto Plata · Samaná
  • E-Ticket — MANDATORY (entry AND exit)Free, at eticket.migracion.gob.do, up to 72 hours before your flight. It generates a QR code to show at boarding and at immigration. Without it: a fine of about US$80.
  • ⚠️ Watch out for fake sitesThe real E-Ticket is 100% free. Third-party sites charge for a service that costs nothing — only use the official portal.
  • Tourist card (~US$10)Allows a 30-day stay. Usually already included in the price of your plane ticket.
  • Valid passport6 months of validity after your return recommended.
  • Medical insuranceRequired for entry. It is not included in your package: buy it separately or check your credit card's coverage.

Mexico

Cancún · Riviera Maya · Playa del Carmen · Costa Mujeres · Cozumel · Puerto Vallarta · Riviera Nayarit · Los Cabos · Huatulco
  • Valid passportfor your entire stay (no strict 6-month rule, but we recommend it).
  • Digital immigration form (FMMN) — on arrivalWhen flying in, you fill it out online on arrival; often at a kiosk where you scan your passport and get a receipt showing the number of days allowed (up to 180). Keep it: you may be asked for it again when you leave.
  • No visa for Canadiansfor a tourist stay of under 180 days.
  • Visitax (Quintana Roo)Cancún and the Riviera Maya are in Quintana Roo, which charges a tourist tax (Visitax) payable online. Check whether it's included in your package. Note: the Pacific coast (Puerto Vallarta, Riviera Nayarit, Los Cabos, Huatulco) is NOT in Quintana Roo — so no Visitax there.

Jamaica

Negril · Ocho Rios · Montego Bay
  • Valid passport6 months of validity recommended, with at least one blank page.
  • C5 "Enter Jamaica" form — MANDATORYFill it out online before your flight, for each traveller (adults and kids). It generates a confirmation to show on arrival.
  • ⚠️ Watch out for fake sitesThe official portal is enterjamaica.gov.jm. Third-party sites charge for this free service.
  • On arrivalYou may be asked for your return ticket (proof of onward travel) and your hotel booking.
  • No visa for Canadiansfor a tourist stay. Travel insurance strongly recommended — private healthcare is expensive.

Bahamas

Nassau · Paradise Island
  • Valid passportat least 3 months beyond your planned departure date; 6 months recommended (your airline may be stricter).
  • Immigration card — on arrivalFill out and sign on arrival; it gets stamped and you keep a portion until you leave. No mandatory online form.
  • No visafor a tourist stay (up to 8 months).
  • Departure tax (~US$20)for ages 6 and up — usually already included in your plane ticket.
  • On arrivalYou may be asked for your return ticket, hotel booking and proof of funds.

Cuba

Varadero · Cayos · Holguín
  • Valid passport6 months of validity after your return recommended.
  • E-visa (electronic visa) — MANDATORYOnline on the official site evisacuba.cu (~CA$7, 90 days, single entry). Replaces the old paper tourist card. Often already included in your package.
  • D’Viajeros form — MANDATORYOnline within 72 hours before arrival; generates a QR code to show starting at boarding (airlines may refuse you without it).
  • Medical insurance — MANDATORYProof may be requested on arrival; not included in your package: buy it separately or check your credit card's coverage.
  • Bring cashSmall bills in CAD or USD: cards often don't work well, especially American cards.
  • ⚠️ Good to knowA trip to Cuba makes you ineligible for the U.S. ESTA: you'll then need a U.S. visa to enter the United States.

Costa Rica

Guanacaste · Liberia
  • Valid passportfor your entire stay; 6 months recommended, with one blank page.
  • No visa for Canadianstourist stay (often 90 days, at the immigration officer's discretion).
  • Onward ticketproof of return or onward travel required on arrival.
  • Departure tax (~US$29)often already included in your plane ticket — check.

Aruba

Palm Beach · Oranjestad
  • Valid passportfor your stay; 6 months recommended.
  • No visatourist stay up to 90 days.
  • ED card (Embarkation/Disembarkation) — MANDATORYOnline within 7 days before departure, on the official site edcardaruba.aw; generates a QR code to show.
  • Sustainability fee (US$20)paid directly when filling out the ED card.
  • On arrivala return ticket and proof of accommodation may be requested.

Curaçao

Willemstad
  • Valid passport6 months of validity recommended.
  • No visatourist stay.
  • Digital Immigration Card (DI Card) — MANDATORYOnline within 7 days before departure, free, on the official site dicardcuracao.com.
  • On arrivala return ticket and proof of accommodation may be requested.

Saint Lucia

Castries · Soufrière
  • Valid passportfor your stay; airlines recommend 6 months.
  • No visa for Canadianstourist stay.
  • Electronic immigration form — MANDATORYone per family/group, online within 3 days before arrival.
  • Onward ticketproof of return travel required.

Saint-Martin / Sint Maarten

Sint Maarten · Saint-Martin
  • Valid passportfor your stay.
  • No visa for Canadiansup to 90 days per year.
  • Sint Maarten entry form (entry.sx) — MANDATORYED card to fill out online before departure (Dutch side).
  • Return ticket + accommodationproof required at entry.

Antigua and Barbuda

Antigua · Barbuda
  • Valid passport6 months recommended.
  • No visa for Canadianstourist stay up to 6 months.
  • Return ticket + accommodation + fundsmay be requested on arrival.

Grenada

St George's · Grand Anse
  • Valid passportfor your stay.
  • No visa for Canadianstourist stay.
  • Onward ticketproof of return travel.

Panama

Playa Blanca · Pacific Riviera
  • Valid passportfor your stay.
  • No visa for Canadianstourist stay.
  • Electronic customs/immigration declarationonline form on entry and exit.
  • Proof of funds + onward ticketmay be requested.

Colombia

Cartagena · Bocagrande
  • Passport valid 6 monthsbeyond your departure date from Colombia.
  • No visa for Canadianstourist stay.
  • Check-Mig — MANDATORYonline pre-registration before entry AND exit.
  • Travel insurancestrongly recommended (private healthcare is expensive).
  • Good to knowhigh degree of caution for the country overall; touristic Cartagena remains safe with the usual precautions.

Belize

Ambergris · Caye Caulker · Placencia
  • Passport valid 30+ daysairlines recommend 6 months.
  • No visa for Canadiansup to 30 days (can be extended).
  • Travel Declaration / iDeclare — MANDATORYonline form (QR) before arrival.
  • Onward ticket + fundsabout US$75/day, on request.
  • Departure tax ~US$55often included in your package.

Honduras / Roatán

Roatán · Bay Islands
  • Passport valid 6 monthsbeyond your departure date.
  • No visa for Canadiansup to 90 days.
  • Good to knowhigh degree of caution for the country overall; Roatán and the Bay Islands are the safer tourist area — stay in the resort zone and choose reputable operators.

Turks & Caicos Islands

Grace Bay · Providenciales
  • Passport valid for the length of your stayrule specific to Canadians.
  • No visa for Canadiansup to 90 days (often 30 granted).
  • Return ticketproof of onward travel.
