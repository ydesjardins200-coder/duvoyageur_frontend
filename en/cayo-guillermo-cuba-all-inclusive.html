Practical guide

Planning your trip to Cayo Guillermo

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Hurricanes mainly from August to October. Cuba's north coast is generally spared from sargassum.

What to do there?

  • Playa Pilar — often called Cuba's most beautiful beach: dunes and turquoise water.
  • Cayo Media Luna sandbar — a boat trip out to a sandbar, plus snorkelling.
  • Pink flamingos — the nearby lagoon is home to flamingo colonies.
  • Diving & snorkelling — the world's second-largest coral reef isn't far.
  • Cayo Coco — the neighbouring cay, with its beaches and hotels, reachable by road.

The real questions

Cayo Guillermo: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

Cayo Guillermo or Cayo Coco?

The two cays are connected by road. Cayo Guillermo is smaller and quieter, with the famous Playa Pilar; Cayo Coco has more hotels. We'll help you choose.

What documents do I need for Cuba?

A passport (6 months' validity recommended), an e-visa via evisacuba.cu (often included) and D'Viajeros within 72 hours before departure. Medical insurance is mandatory. See our Documents and passport guide.

Is there sargassum?

Cuba's north coast is generally spared from sargassum.

Is it safe right now?

Canada has advised a "high degree of caution" in Cuba since January 2026 (shortages). We'll help you pick a reliable resort.
