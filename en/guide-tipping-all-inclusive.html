Our pro tips:
  • Bring your tips in small US dollar bills ($1 and $5) — exchange before you leave, it's easier once you're there.
  • Plan on about US$40–60 per person for a week, and adjust to your style.
  • Tipping is optional and personal — it's a gesture of appreciation, never an obligation.
  • A small tip for the bartender or housekeeper on day one often makes the whole stay better.

Suggested amounts are for guidance only, based on common practice at all-inclusives in the Caribbean and Mexico. CAD conversion is approximate. Adjust based on your experience and generosity.
