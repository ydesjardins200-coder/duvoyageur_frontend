Practical guide

Planning your trip to Belize

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Diving and snorkelling on the barrier reef are superb all year round. Hurricanes mostly from August to October.

What to do there?

  • Barrier Reef & Blue Hole — the 2nd-largest in the world: exceptional diving and snorkelling.
  • Caye Caulker — the laid-back "Go Slow" island, perfect for unplugging.
  • Ambergris Caye / San Pedro — the biggest caye, with restaurants, diving and a lively scene.
  • Mayan temples — Xunantunich, Caracol and Lamanai in the jungle.
  • Caves & underground rivers — tubing and exploring in the backcountry.

The real questions

Belize: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Belize?

About 5 hours, on a seasonal direct flight or with a connection. Then a water taxi to the cayes (San Pedro, Caye Caulker).

What documents do I need for Belize?

A valid passport (30+ days, 6 months recommended), no visa for Canadians (stays of 30 days or less), the mandatory online Travel Declaration (iDeclare) form, and a departure tax of about US$55 (often included). See our Documents and passport guide.

Is Belize an all-inclusive destination?

Not really: it's mostly boutique hotels and dive lodges. We'll help you put together a stay that fits your style.

Is it safe?

The tourist areas (Ambergris, Caye Caulker, Placencia) are safe. We recommend caution at night in Belize City.
