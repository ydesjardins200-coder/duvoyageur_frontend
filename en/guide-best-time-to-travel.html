🇲🇽 Mexico — Cancún / Riviera Maya

Sunny and dry from December to April: prime time. Sargassum (seaweed) mostly affects the Caribbean coast from spring to summer, and varies from day to day.

Ideal: Dec → April

🐋 Mexico — Pacific Coast (Vallarta, Los Cabos, Huatulco)

Dry season from November to April, with the best weather. No sargassum here: it's the Pacific. And from December to March, humpback whales visit Banderas Bay and Los Cabos.

Ideal: Nov → April · whales Dec → March

🇩🇴 Dominican Republic — Punta Cana

Warm year-round; December to April is still the most pleasant. The east coast can get sargassum in spring and summer depending on the currents.

Ideal: Dec → April

🐋 Dominican Rep. — Puerto Plata / Samaná

The north: same lovely dry season, generally less affected by sargassum. And from mid-January to late March, humpback whales parade through Samaná Bay.

Whales: mid-Jan → late March

🇯🇲 Jamaica — Negril & Ocho Rios

December to April: dry season and the best weather. The west coast (Negril) sees little sargassum; the north coast (Ocho Rios) stays lush and green. Hurricanes mostly from August to October.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇧🇸 Bahamas — Nassau

December to April: dry, sunny season. Since it's farther north, winter is a bit cooler and windier than further south, but still pleasant. Little sargassum; hurricanes mostly from August to October.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇨🇺 Cuba — Varadero / Cayos

December to April: dry season and the best weather. The northern cayos (Cayo Coco, Santa María) are generally spared from sargassum. Hurricanes mostly from August to October.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇨🇷 Costa Rica — Guanacaste

On the Pacific side, the dry season runs from December to April: the best weather. The green season (May to November) is rainier, but lush and cheaper.

Dry (Pacific): Dec → April

🇦🇼 Aruba & Curaçao (ABC Islands)

The big advantage: these islands are OUTSIDE the hurricane belt, dry and sunny all year long. A steady breeze keeps things cool even in summer. You can go anytime.

Ideal: year-round

🇱🇨 Saint Lucia

December to April: dry season and the best weather. The rainy season (June to November) brings short showers. The island stays green and spectacular all year long.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇸🇽 Saint-Martin / Sint Maarten

Dry, sunny season from December to April. One island, two vibes (fine dining on the French side, nightlife on the Dutch side). Hurricanes mostly from August to October.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇦🇬 Antigua

Lovely dry season from December to April, with a steady wind that makes it a sailing paradise. 365 beaches to choose from. Hurricanes mostly from August to October.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇬🇩 Grenada

The Spice Isle lies south of the hurricane belt, so it's less exposed. Dry season from December to April, lush nature year-round.

Ideal: Dec → April · outside hurricane belt

🇵🇦 Panama — Pacific Riviera (Playa Blanca)

Dry season from December to April: the best weather. The rainy season (May to November) stays warm, with showers mostly in the afternoon.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇨🇴 Colombia — Cartagena

Cartagena is hot all year; December to April is the driest and most pleasant time. Above all, a destination for cultural discovery.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇧🇿 Belize

Dry season from December to April, ideal for combining beaches, cayes and jungle. Superb diving and snorkelling on the barrier reef year-round.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇭🇳 Roatán (Honduras)

Dry season from December to April, with the best weather. Diving on the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef is superb almost all year long.

Ideal: Dec → April

🇹🇨 Turks & Caicos Islands

Sunny and dry for much of the year; December to April is still the most pleasant. Grace Bay is a must. Hurricanes mostly from August to October.

Ideal: Dec → April

💡 Pro tips

  • Book early for the holidays and March Break: they're the priciest periods and they sell out fast.
  • The best deals are in the low seasons: May, September–October and early December.
  • Leave midweek when you can: Tuesday/Wednesday departures are often cheaper.
  • Hurricane season (June–Nov): get insurance with cancellation/interruption coverage.
  • Sargassum: it changes from day to day and beach to beach — no destination is affected all the time.
