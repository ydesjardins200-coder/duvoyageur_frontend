How to head down south without breaking the bank — here are the answers to the most common questions.

What are the best-value all-inclusives down south?

It depends on your destination: in Mexico, we love Barceló Maya, Iberostar (Paraíso, Selection Cancún), Riu Palace and Dreams Jade; in the Dominican Republic, Bahia Principe, Royalton and Majestic Elegance in Punta Cana; in Cuba, Iberostar Grand Packard, Blau Varadero and Ocean Vista Azul; and the Sonesta Maho Beach in St. Martin. They all offer excellent value for money.

How do I find a cheap all-inclusive without sacrificing quality?

Three levers: book early (or last minute), travel outside peak weeks, and stay flexible on dates. We compare prices across tour operators for you and apply our discount on top — you get the best price without shopping around yourself.

What is the cheapest time to travel down south?

Outside the dry high season (December to April), prices often drop: May to early June, then September–October, are usually the cheapest and least crowded.
