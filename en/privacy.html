Privacy Policy

Please note. This document is provided for information purposes and is a template adapted to the context of a Québec travel agency. It does not replace legal advice. Before final publication, have it reviewed by a legal advisor and make sure it reflects your actual practices.

01 Scope

This policy applies to the duvoyageur.ca website, our Facebook page and our Messenger chat service, as well as to any communication you exchange with us to plan, book or change a trip.

It is governed by Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (as modernized by Québec Law 25) and, where applicable, by Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

02 Person in charge of the protection of personal information

A person in charge of the protection of personal information ensures compliance with this policy within Du Voyageur. You can reach them with any question, access request or complaint using the contact details in the “Contact us” section.

03 Information we collect

Depending on the service requested, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details: last name, first name, email address, phone number, city.
  • Travel information: destination, dates, number of travellers, desired package or hotel, preferences and special needs.
  • Information required by suppliers: date of birth and, where the booking requires it, information shown on your travel documents (for example, your passport).
  • Payment information: processed securely by our suppliers and financial institutions; we do not keep your full card numbers.
  • Communications: the content of your messages sent by form, email or Messenger.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and cookie data (see section 9).

04 How we obtain your information

We collect your information mainly directly from you: when you fill out our request form, write to us on Messenger, call us or correspond with us. Some technical data is collected automatically as you browse the site.

05 Why we use your information

  • To prepare a quote and offer you the best price on your package;
  • To make, confirm, change or cancel a booking with suppliers;
  • To communicate with you about your file and answer your questions;
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations;
  • To improve our website and services;
  • To send you, with your consent, offers and news that may interest you.

06 Consent

By providing your information, you consent to its use for the purposes described above. For promotional communications, your consent is requested separately and you may withdraw it at any time, including through the unsubscribe link or by writing to us. Withdrawing consent is not retroactive and may, in some cases, limit our ability to provide you with a service.

07 Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We disclose it only when necessary to provide the service, including to:

  • travel suppliers (tour operators, airlines, hotels, cruise lines) required to carry out your booking;
  • our technology service providers, including the Softvoyage/Tripbook booking platform and our web host;
  • payment processors;
  • the competent authorities, when required by law.

These third parties may use your information only for the agreed purposes.

08 Transfers outside Québec

Some of our suppliers or the services booked (hotels, carriers) may be located outside Québec or Canada. When personal information must be disclosed there, we take reasonable measures to ensure it receives adequate protection, as required by applicable law.

09 Cookies and browsing data

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to function properly, remember your preferences and measure overall traffic. You can set your browser to block or delete cookies; however, some features may be affected.

10 Retention and destruction

We keep your information as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations. When it is no longer required, it is securely destroyed or anonymized.

11 Security

We implement reasonable security measures — technical, physical and organizational — to protect your information against loss, theft and unauthorized access. Since no system is foolproof, we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to responding promptly in the event of a confidentiality incident.

12 Your rights

Subject to the conditions set out by law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • have corrected any information that is inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous;
  • withdraw your consent to certain uses;
  • request that we stop disseminating or de-index information in the cases provided for by law.

To exercise a right, write to us using the contact details in section 15. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

13 Information about minors

Our website is intended for adults. A minor traveller's information is collected only from a parent or guardian, solely for the purposes of the booking concerned.

14 Changes to this policy

We may change this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The version in effect is always the one published on this page, with its last-updated date.
