Practical guide

Getting ready for your Playa Blanca (Panama) trip

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. The rainy season (May to November) stays hot, with showers mostly in the afternoon. Pair your beach time with a day in Panama City and its canal.

What to do there?

  • Playa Blanca beach — a long golden-sand beach, perfect for unwinding.
  • Panama Canal — one of the great engineering marvels, on a day trip to Panama City.
  • Panama's Old Town (Casco Viejo) — a UNESCO-listed colonial quarter with restaurants and rooftops.
  • El Valle de Antón — a village inside a volcanic crater, with a market and nature.
  • Islands & snorkelling — boat trips to small Pacific islands.

Real questions

Playa Blanca (Panama): what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Panama (Playa Blanca)?

About 5 h 30 on a seasonal direct flight to Río Hato airport, just 10-20 min from the Playa Blanca resorts.

What documents do I need for Panama?

A valid passport (no visa for Canadians), proof of funds and an onward or return ticket, plus an electronic customs/immigration declaration to fill out. See our Documents and passport guide.

Can I visit the Panama Canal?

Yes — Panama City, its canal and Casco Viejo make a great day trip from Playa Blanca.

When is the best time to go?

December to April for the dry season. See our Best time to go guide.
