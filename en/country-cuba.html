Must-sees

Cuba's top highlights

🚗
Havana & its vintage cars

A trip back in time: the malecón, the old town and street music.

🏛️
Trinidad

A colonial gem frozen in time, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

🏖️
Cayo beaches

Cayo Coco, Cayo Largo, Varadero: unreal turquoise water.

🚬
Viñales Valley

Tobacco plantations and karst landscapes (mogotes).

🎶
Salsa & Cuban son

Music is everywhere — it's the soul of the island.

🐠
Diving

Jardines de la Reina and María la Gorda, among the best spots in the Caribbean.
