Practical guide

Planning your trip to the north of the Dominican Republic

When to go?

December to April is the dry season, with the best weather. If you're after the whales in Samaná, mid-January to late March is peak season. The rainy and hurricane season runs mostly from August to October.

What to do there?

  • Whale watching in Samaná — from January to March, on a boat excursion in the bay.
  • Damajagua Falls — the famous "27 Charcos" to jump and slide down, near Puerto Plata.
  • Mount Isabel de Torres cable car — panoramic views over Puerto Plata and the coast.
  • Cayo Levantado & El Limón — "Bacardi Island" and a 40 m waterfall, on the Samaná side.
  • Cabarete & Sosúa — beaches and surf spots on the north coast.

The real questions

Travelling to the Dominican north coast: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive in Puerto Plata or Samaná, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Puerto Plata?

About 4 h to 4 h 30 on a direct flight, from Montréal airport (YUL) to Puerto Plata (POP). Direct flights are available most days of the week.

When can you see the whales in Samaná?

From mid-January to late March, peaking in February. Every year, thousands of humpback whales come to breed in Samaná Bay; you can watch them on a boat excursion from the port. It's one of the best places in the world for it.

What documents do I need for the Dominican Republic?

A valid Canadian passport (until December 31, 2026, valid for the length of your stay for tourism; many airlines require 6 months). You also need to fill out the online E-Ticket — a form that generates a QR code, mandatory on arrival and departure, which can be completed up to 120 days in advance. No visa required. Always check the latest requirements.

What's the best time to visit the Dominican north coast?

December to April is the dry season, with the best weather. For the whales in Samaná, aim for January to March. The rainy and hurricane season runs mostly from August to October.

What currency should I use?

The official currency is the Dominican peso (DOP), but US dollars and credit cards are widely accepted at resorts. Let your bank know before you leave if you plan to use your cards.
