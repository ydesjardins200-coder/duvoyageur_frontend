What you can bring back to Canada

Rum, cigars, souvenirs… how much can you bring home without paying taxes? The CBSA rules, explained simply.

🍷 Alcohol

Only one of these options
  • 1.5 L of wine (≈ 2 bottles)
  • or 1.14 L of spirits (one large bottle of rum)
  • or 8.5 L of beer (≈ 24 cans)

Only allowed after 48 h away, and if you meet the legal drinking age of the province where you enter.

🚬 Tobacco

  • 200 cigarettes
  • 50 cigars
  • 200 g of manufactured tobacco
  • 200 tobacco sticks

After 48 h, ages 18 and up. A special duty may apply if products aren't stamped "DUTY PAID CANADA".

⚠️ Cannabis is still prohibited at the border — in both directions, even though it's legal in Canada. Never cross with cannabis, edibles or CBD oil: it's a serious criminal offence.

For information purposes only, based on the rules of the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA). Rules and amounts may change — check the official source before you leave: cbsa-asfc.gc.ca.
