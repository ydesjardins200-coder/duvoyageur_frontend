💡 Pro tips

  • Baggage weight: check the allowance for your package (often 1 suitcase of about 23 kg + 1 carry-on). Weigh your suitcase at home to avoid fees.
  • In the cabin: liquids of 100 ml max in a clear 1 L bag; power banks and electronics always with you, never in checked luggage.
  • Document safety: take photos of your passport, tickets and insurance, and back them up to the cloud.
  • At the airport: arrive 3 hours before an international flight. Keep your hotel address handy for the customs form.
  • Sunscreen: for Mexico's cenotes and eco-parks, biodegradable sunscreen is often mandatory.
