The real questions

Family all-inclusives: what people ask us

Before you book your family sun vacation, here are the answers to the questions we hear most often.

Which all-inclusive chains are best for families?

We recommend Dreams, Barceló, Riu, Iberostar and Bahia Principe. They're safe bets at good prices, with entertainment, kids' clubs and services designed for families.

Do kids travel free or at a discount?

Many packages offer a child discount, and some hotels welcome little ones for free — but it varies by hotel, tour operator and travel dates. Tell us your kids' ages and we'll find the best deal for your family.

What services should I look for on a vacation with kids?

A kids' club, waterslides or a water park, daytime entertainment, family rooms and a variety of dining options. Tell us your kids' ages and we'll point you to the best-suited hotels.

What's the best time to travel as a family?

March break and summer are popular but more expensive. To save, aim for off-peak periods when the school calendar allows. We'll help you find the right balance between dates and price.

Do I pay more with a discount agency?

No, it's the opposite. You pay less than the price listed by the tour operator, because we give you back part of our commission as a discount, applied directly to your price.

Is it safe to book with Du Voyageur?

Yes. We hold Québec travel agency licence no. 700495 and book through the official Softvoyage network. Your booking is a real booking with the tour operator (Transat, Air Canada Vacations, Sunwing, WestJet).
