Practical guide

Planning your Grenada trip

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Grenada lies south of the hurricane belt, so it's less exposed than many islands. Lush nature all year long.

What to do there?

  • Grand Anse Beach — 3 km of white sand, one of the most beautiful beaches in the Caribbean.
  • St George's — the horseshoe-shaped harbour and its colourful houses, one of the prettiest in the Antilles.
  • Spice plantations — nutmeg, cocoa and cinnamon: the soul of the Spice Isle.
  • Underwater Sculpture Park — diving or snorkelling among submerged statues.
  • Waterfalls & rainforest — Annandale, Seven Sisters and Grand Étang park.

Real questions

Grenada: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most frequently asked questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Grenada?

About 5 h, on a seasonal direct flight or with a connection, then a 20–40 min drive.

What documents do I need for Grenada?

A valid passport and a return ticket. No visa for Canadians. See our Documents and passport guide.

Who is Grenada for?

For travellers looking for an authentic, nature-filled, less touristy island, with superb beaches and a laid-back vibe.

Are there hurricanes?

Grenada lies south of the main hurricane belt, so it's less exposed. The riskiest season is mostly August to October.
