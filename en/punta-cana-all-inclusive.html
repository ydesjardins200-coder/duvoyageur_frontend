Practical guide

Planning your Punta Cana trip

When to go?

The dry season, from December to April, has the best weather — it's also the busiest time. To pay less and enjoy quieter beaches, aim for May to early June. The rainy and hurricane season mostly runs from August to October.

What to do there?

  • Saona Island — a catamaran excursion to a paradise island, the great classic.
  • Hoyo Azul & Scape Park — a turquoise cenote and adventures in the great outdoors.
  • Bávaro Beach — miles of white sand lined with coconut palms.
  • Catamaran & snorkelling — to explore the Caribbean's turquoise waters.
  • Altos de Chavón — a hilltop Mediterranean-style village, perfect for a day trip.

The real questions

Punta Cana trips: what people ask us

Before you book your Punta Cana all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Punta Cana?

About 4 to 4.5 hours non-stop, from Montréal airport (YUL) to Punta Cana airport (PUJ). It's one of the quickest sun destinations to reach from Québec.

What documents do I need for Punta Cana?

A valid Canadian passport (until December 31, 2026, it must be valid for the length of your stay for tourism; however, several airlines require 6 months). You also need to fill out the E-Ticket online — a form that generates a QR code, mandatory on arrival and departure, which can be completed up to 120 days ahead. No visa required. Always check the latest requirements before you leave.

When is the best time to go to Punta Cana?

December to April is the dry season: the best weather, but also the highest prices. May to early June often offers better rates and smaller crowds. The rainy and hurricane season mostly runs from August to October.

What currency should I use in Punta Cana?

The official currency is the Dominican peso (DOP), but US dollars, credit cards and debit cards are widely accepted at resorts. Let your bank know before you leave if you plan to use your cards.

What are the best all-inclusives in Punta Cana?

Among our favourites: the Bahia Principe Grand Punta Cana and the Royalton Punta Cana for families, and the Majestic Elegance Punta Cana for couples. We also have the Bahia Principe Luxury Esmeralda and many more in our best-value picks.
