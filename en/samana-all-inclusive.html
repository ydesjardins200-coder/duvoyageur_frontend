Practical guide

Planning your Samaná trip

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. The highlight: from mid-January to mid-March, thousands of humpback whales come to breed in Samaná Bay.

What to do there?

  • Whale watching — mid-January to mid-March: one of the best places in the world to see humpback whales.
  • El Limón waterfall — a 40 m falls deep in the jungle, on foot or on horseback.
  • Cayo Levantado — “Bacardi Island,” a paradise beach day.
  • Los Haitises National Park — mangroves, caves and Taíno art, by boat.
  • Playa Rincón — regularly ranked among the most beautiful beaches in the Caribbean.

The real questions

Samaná: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are the answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Samaná?

About 4 hours non-stop (mostly in winter) to El Catey airport (AZS), then 30–45 min to the resorts.

What documents do I need for the Dominican Republic?

A valid passport and the online “E-Ticket” form before arrival. See our Documents and passport guide.

When can I see whales in Samaná?

From mid-January to mid-March — it's THE whale-watching destination in the Dominican Republic.

Samaná or Punta Cana?

Samaná is greener, more natural and quieter (whales, waterfalls); Punta Cana has more hotels and more going on. We'll help you choose.
