Must-sees

Dominican Republic highlights

🏖️
Playa Bávaro & Punta Cana

Miles of white sand and palm trees — the beach heart of the DR.

🐋
Samaná's whales

From mid-January to mid-March, one of the best spots in the world.

🏛️
Santo Domingo's Zona Colonial

The oldest colonial city in the Americas (UNESCO).

🏝️
Saona Island

The postcard beach excursion: turquoise water and starfish.

⛰️
Jarabacoa & Pico Duarte

Mountains, rivers and the highest peak in the Caribbean.

🥃
Rum, cigars & merengue

Dominican culture: warm and festive.
