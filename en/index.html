Our model

Why book with us?

When you book an all-inclusive package, the tour operator — Transat, Sunwing Vacations, Air Canada Vacations — pays a commission to the agency handling your booking. Most agencies keep that whole commission. We give you most of it back, as a discount that rewards you for your research, WITHOUT losing the support of our agents.

  1. ＄

    The tour operator pays a commission

    On every package booked, the tour operator pays the agency a commission.

  2. ↔

    We share it with you

    Instead of keeping it all, we give you most of that commission back.

  3. 🌴

    You travel for less

    Same package, same support from our agents.

Nothing magic, nothing shady: we're an agency holding Québec travel agency licence no. 700495 and we book through the official Softvoyage network. Your booking is a real booking, made with the tour operator.

Shop like a pro

Where to find your package

The secret is to shop around yourself. Compare public prices on the major sites in Québec, then look up the same package in your member area: your Reward Discount is already applied there.

Transat transat.com Québec tour operator, Air Transat flights. Sun and Europe packages. Sunwing Vacations sunwing.ca Huge choice of all-inclusive hotels in the Caribbean, Mexico and Cuba. Air Canada Vacations vacancesaircanada.com Packages with Air Canada flights, to sun destinations everywhere. WestJet Vacations westjet.com All-inclusive packages with WestJet flights to sun destinations.

What to write down

  • Exact hotel
  • Dates (departure and return)
  • Departure airport
  • Travellers (adults, kids + ages)
  • Price shown (per person or total)
  • Tour operator

Tip: compare a few sites for the same hotel — prices often change from one day to the next.
