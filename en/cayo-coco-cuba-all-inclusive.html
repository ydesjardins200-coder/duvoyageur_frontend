Practical guide

Planning your Cayo Coco trip

When to go?

December to April for the dry season and the best weather. Hurricanes are mainly a risk from August to October. The cay is peaceful year-round, with long, uncrowded beaches.

What to do there?

  • Playa Pilar (Cayo Guillermo) — often ranked among Cuba's most beautiful beaches, right next to Cayo Coco.
  • Pink flamingos — the lagoon between the mainland and the cay is home to flamingo colonies.
  • Snorkelling and diving — reefs you can reach straight from the beach.
  • Catamaran excursion — out to sandbars and snorkelling spots.
  • Day trip to Morón — a glimpse of real Cuban life, away from the resorts.

Real questions

Cayo Coco: what people ask us

Before you book your all-inclusive, here are answers to the most common questions.

How long is the flight from Montréal to Cayo Coco?

About 3 h 30 on a direct flight (mostly in winter). The airport is on the cay, so the transfer is very short (15–30 min).

What documents do I need for Cuba?

A passport (6 months recommended), an e-visa from the official site evisacuba.cu (often included in your package) and the D'Viajeros form within 72 hours before departure. Medical insurance is mandatory. See our Passport and travel documents guide.

Is there sargassum in Cayo Coco?

Cuba's north-coast beaches are generally spared from sargassum, unlike some coastlines in Mexico.

Is it safe right now?

Canada has advised "a high degree of caution" in Cuba since January 2026 because of shortages that can affect resorts. We stay transparent and point you to a reliable hotel.
